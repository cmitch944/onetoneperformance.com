Is this hypnotherapy?

No. The OneTone Performance System™ is not therapy and not hypnotherapy. It doesn't diagnose, label, or treat a condition. It's a performance system: it uses presupposition, NLP, and hypnotic patterns to change the belief an event installed — the belief that's been running the behavior. Different work, different aim.

Is this hypnosis? Will I lose control?

The system uses hypnotic patterns as one of its tools — the same way a great conversation can change how you see something. You are not “put under,” and nothing happens against your own stated goal. The tools serve the change you came for; the answers that surface are yours.

The smoking track is designed as one deep session. That's the design — go to the moment, change the belief, leave as a non-smoker. It's a design, not a promise about any one person's outcome.
